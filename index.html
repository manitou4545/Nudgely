How it works

1. Add your invoice
Client, amount, due date. Made in PayPal, Canva or Word, it does not matter.
2. Nudgely reminds your client
3 days before, on the due date, then 7 and 15 days after. Tone gets firmer each time.
3. You get paid
Replies go straight to your inbox. Mark the invoice paid and reminders stop.
