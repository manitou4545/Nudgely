Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Nudgely is run by Gérard Nomo, based in Cameroon. Contact: nudgely0@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your account email and password (the password is stored in protected form by our provider). The payment instructions you choose to save, and the invoices you add: client names, client emails, optional client phone numbers, invoice numbers, amounts, currencies, due dates and status. If you ask to upgrade: the payment method, the transaction ID and your email. If you enable notifications: your browser’s push subscription (an address used to deliver notifications) and your time zone.

Why we use it

To run the service, show you your invoices, prepare reminders that you send yourself, activate Pro after a payment and answer your questions. We do not sell your data and we show no ads.

Who processes it

Your data is stored with Supabase. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you send a payment request, a notification with your email, payment method and transaction ID is sent to the operator through the ntfy service.

Your clients' data

You are responsible for having the right to store your clients' names and emails and to contact them about their invoices.

Browser storage

Your browser's local storage keeps your login session and small settings. We do not use advertising cookies.

Deleting your data

Email us and we will delete your account and your data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes.
