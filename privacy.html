Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Nudgely is run by Gérard Nomo, an individual based in Cameroon, who decides how your data is used (the “controller”). Contact: nudgely0@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: email, password (stored in protected form by our provider), optional name, profile photo and language.
  • Your invoices: client names, client emails, optional WhatsApp numbers, invoice numbers, amounts, currencies, due dates, reminder and payment status.
  • Settings: the payment instructions you save, notification subscription details, referral links and rewards.
  • Payments to us: payment method, transaction ID, date and status of your Pro payment requests.
  • Technical data: error logs (error message, page, browser type, app version, account ID). They contain no invoice or client data.

Why we use it, and on what basis

To provide the Service you asked for (contract); to check payments and activate Pro (contract); to keep the Service secure and fix bugs (legitimate interest); and to send morning notifications to your device only if you turn them on (consent, which you can withdraw at any time). We do not sell your data, we show no ads and we do not profile you.

Who processes it

  • Supabase: database, login and photo storage.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.
  • ntfy.sh: delivers a private alert to the operator when you send a payment request (your email, payment method and transaction ID).
  • Payoneer or your crypto network: process your payment; we do not receive your card details.
  • Your browser's push service (for example Google, Apple or Mozilla): delivers notifications you turned on.
  • Resend: only if we turn on automatic email reminders; we will update this policy first.

Your data may be stored and processed outside your country, including outside the EEA, under the safeguards of these providers. Nudgely does not itself send reminders to your clients: you send them from your own email or messaging app.

Your clients' data

You are responsible for having the right to store your clients' names and contact details and to contact them about their invoices. We process this data only for you and on your instructions.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Deleting your account from the Account tab permanently deletes your profile, invoices, clients, settings, payment requests, referral data and error logs. Technical backups may keep copies for a short time. We may keep payment records that we are required to keep by law or for accounting.

Your rights

You can access and correct your data in the app, export your invoices with the CSV export, and delete your account yourself. You can also write to us to ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, or to object to processing. If you are in the EU or UK you can complain to your data protection authority. We answer within 30 days.

Security

Access to data is protected by login and access rules so that each account sees only its own data. No online service is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you we will tell you and the authorities where the law requires it.

Browser storage

Your browser's local storage keeps your login session, your language and small settings. We do not use advertising cookies.

Children

Nudgely is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes, and we will tell you in the app for significant changes.
