Plans and payment

The Free plan allows up to 3 unpaid invoices at a time. Pro costs 5 per month and allows unlimited invoices. You can pay in USDT on the TRC20 network, by bank transfer or with Orange Money. After you confirm your payment, we check it and activate Pro, usually within 24 hours. Each payment covers one month.

Crypto sent on the wrong network cannot be recovered. Please check the network and address before you send.
